Another project I was responsible for during my time at Alpar was an Ionic mobile application, for another big company that already had a web-based application for managing HR related requests, such as vacations and sick days. The company ordered a low-budget simple app for managers to get notifications on their mobile when an employee requested anything. The solution was an Ionic application that loads the web-based responsive website in a WebView and manages, through Firebase, the devices and authentication information for each user — sending push notifications when necessary.
