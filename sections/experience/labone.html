"Metrô" is the subway system of São Paulo city. Metrô came to LabOne looking for a digital signage solution, which became part of a project that included an interactive kiosk with Google Maps integration, where users could view the map of the subway's proximity, including nearby points of interest — clicking a point of interest on the touchscreen showed the way to get there with detailed instructions. Later, to improve performance, Google Gears was integrated, guaranteeing the system would keep working even if the internet connection was lost.

Technology: C# and .NET MVC for the administration system; Google Maps, Google Gears, JavaScript and jQuery for the front end.
