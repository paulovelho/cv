Savedo is a fintech startup, part of Finleap/Hitfox Group, providing investment products for Germans to invest in other banks throughout Europe. I was hired when the company was in its first year and it was amazing to see the company grow and to help it get there. www.savedo.de
  • Development of a platform for clients to make investments, money transactions, assets control and every interaction with our product.
  • Application development based on TypeScript and Angular 1.0
  • Architecture of APIs and micro-services
  • Ruby on Rails development of back-end and marketing website
  • Use of agile methods for development and OKRs for defining goals

Building the banking application was the biggest task I did during my period at Savedo. From the beginning, we decided to use modern technologies and languages in order to build a platform that could grow healthy and with high-end features. We developed a full front-end application to interact with a back-end API, built with Angular 1.0 and Ionic over TypeScript. The whole project was fully covered with tests provided by the mocha framework, and communication with the servers followed the jsonapi patterns.
The API was built in Ruby on Rails; although I wasn't responsible for the inner engines of the API, it wasn't rare for me to put my hands on the server code to fix or build something.
